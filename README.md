# Privacy Policy — AutoMessage

Last updated: May 2025

This app connects to your Instagram Business account via Meta's official API
to send automated keyword-based replies to direct messages.

## Data We Collect
- Instagram account ID and username
- Facebook Page ID and access token (stored securely)
- Incoming DM text (for keyword matching only)

## How We Use Data
- To match keywords and send predefined replies
- No data is sold or shared with third parties

## Data Deletion
To delete your data, disconnect the app from your Instagram account settings,
or email: tojikdominic@gmail.com
