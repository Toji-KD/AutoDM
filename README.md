# Privacy Policy — AutoMessage

Last updated: May 2025

This app connects to your Instagram Business account via Meta's official API
to send automated keyword-based replies to direct messages.

## Data We Collect
- Instagram account ID and username
- Facebook Page ID and access token (stored securely)
- Incoming DM text (for keyword matching only)

## How We Use Data
- To match keywords and send predefined replies
- No data is sold or shared with third parties

## Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Data
1.Revoke Instagram Access

Open Instagram → Settings → Security → Apps and Websites → find DM Pulse → tap Remove. This immediately revokes our access to your Instagram account.
2.Request Account Deletion

Email us at support@dmpulse.in with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your Instagram username. We will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 7 business days.
3.Confirmation

We will send you a confirmation email once your data has been deleted. After deletion, your data cannot be recovered.
What Gets Deleted

    Your Instagram profile information (username, name, user ID)
    Your stored access token
    All keyword rules you created
    All message logs associated with your account
    Your account settings and default reply

Payment records may be retained for up to 90 days as required by financial regulations, after which they are also permanently deleted.
Contact

For any data deletion queries or concerns:

    Email: support@dmpulse.in
    Response time: Within 2 business days
    Deletion completed: Within 7 business days of request
